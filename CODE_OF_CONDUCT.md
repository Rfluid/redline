# Code of conduct

redline follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@Rfluid](https://github.com/Rfluid) privately (GitHub profile). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
